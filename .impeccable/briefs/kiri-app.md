# Surface brief: KIRI mobile app (camera, result, collection)

Mode: Operate. Platform: adaptive (iOS + Android, Expo).
Audience: Spanish speakers learning English by photographing real objects. Task: shoot, read the word, hear it, save it, browse the collection.
Constraints: native tab bar + native controls, safe areas, system Back/edge-swipe, Reduce Motion honored, light and dark first-class. Spanish UI copy.
Unresolved: quiz/spaced repetition, monetization.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Monochrome product canon: a fine neutral scale with ONE accent used about four times in the whole app; every claim paired with its proof (the word beside the photo that earned it). Refuses the colorful, gamified, mascot language-app default.
OWN-WORLD: near-white #FAFAFA to near-black #0A0A0A in a 10-step neutral scale, hairline 1px borders, soft radial grounds behind photos; accent electric blue #2F6BFF only on: the scan lock, the primary Save fill, the unsaved dot, the audio-playing state. Tight geometric sans (Geist) at two weights for words and headings; system UI face for labels. Ghost hairline secondary buttons, solid accent primary.
STORY: A user understands in one second: point, tap, learn the word. They believe because the proof (their photo) sits next to the claim (the word). They save and watch the collection grow.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed camera. Native tab bar (Cámara, Colección, Perfil). Centered corner-bracket reticle in white; shutter bottom center (72pt ring); gallery thumbnail lower left; language chip upper left (EN). After capture the photo settles into a framed card on a soft radial neutral ground, the word resolves large below (Geist 600, 56pt), Spanish translation and phonetic beneath in neutral-500, ghost "Escuchar" and solid accent "Guardar".
FORM: Monochrome Product Marketing (challenger, digital-design-canon-monochrome-product-marketing), kept as a challenger win from re-roll 1; seed:c3c9827ce41220b6. Signature move: the "proof lock": on shutter the reticle brackets contract onto the subject in accent blue, the photo springs into its framed card and the word types in over a hairline, each step on exponential ease-out springs via Reanimated.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
